## Introduction

Efact is a comprehensive business management and electronic invoicing platform designed for the Peruvian market. It provides a complete solution for managing your business operations, including product management, inventory control, sales and invoicing, customer and supplier management, cash operations, and reporting.

This user manual provides detailed instructions on how to use the various features of the Efact system. Whether you're a new user getting started with the system or an experienced user looking for specific information, this manual will help you make the most of Efact's capabilities.

## Getting Started

### System Requirements

To use Efact, you need:
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
- Internet connection
- Screen resolution of at least 1280x720

### Logging In

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the Efact login page.
2. Enter your username and password.
3. Click the "Login" button.

If you've forgotten your password, click the "Forgot Password" link and follow the instructions to reset it.

### User Interface Overview

The Efact user interface consists of the following main elements:

- **Header**: Contains the company name, current branch, and user information.
- **Side Navigation**: Provides access to all the modules and features of the system.
- **Content Area**: Displays the currently selected module or feature.
- **Footer**: Contains copyright information and the current version of the system.

## Dashboard

The Dashboard provides an overview of your business performance with various charts and metrics.

### Sales Analytics

The Dashboard displays the following sales analytics:

- **Weekly Sales**: Bar chart showing sales for each day of the current week.
- **Monthly Sales**: Line chart showing sales for each day of the current month.
- **Yearly Sales**: Bar chart showing sales for each month of the current year.

### Invoicing Analytics

The Dashboard also displays invoicing analytics:

- **Monthly Invoicing**: Bar chart showing the number of invoices generated each month.

## Product Management

The Product Management module allows you to manage your products, categories, and units of measure.

### Adding Products

To add a new product:

1. Navigate to the Products page.
2. Click the "Add" button.
3. Fill in the product details:
   - **Image**: Upload an image of the product (optional).
   - **Code**: Enter a unique code for the product.
   - **Type**: Select whether the product is physical or a service.
   - **Name**: Enter the name of the product.
   - **Category**: Select the category the product belongs to.
   - **Unit of Measure**: Select the unit of measure for the product.
   - **Price**: Enter the sale price of the product.
4. Click the "Save" button.

### Editing Products

To edit an existing product:

1. Navigate to the Products page.
2. Find the product you want to edit in the list.
3. Click the "Edit" button for that product.
4. Modify the product details as needed.
5. Click the "Save" button.

### Managing Categories

To manage product categories:

1. Navigate to the Categories page.
2. To add a new category, click the "Add" button and fill in the category details.
3. To edit a category, find it in the list, click the "Edit" button, modify the details, and click "Save".
4. To delete a category, find it in the list, click the "Delete" button, and confirm the deletion.

### Managing Units of Measure

To manage units of measure:

1. Navigate to the Units of Measure page.
2. To add a new unit, click the "Add" button and fill in the unit details.
3. To edit a unit, find it in the list, click the "Edit" button, modify the details, and click "Save".
4. To delete a unit, find it in the list, click the "Delete" button, and confirm the deletion.

### Price Lists

Price lists allow you to define different prices for different customers or scenarios.

To manage price lists:

1. Navigate to the Price Lists page.
2. To add a new price list, click the "Add" button and fill in the price list details.
3. To edit a price list, find it in the list, click the "Edit" button, modify the details, and click "Save".
4. To delete a price list, find it in the list, click the "Delete" button, and confirm the deletion.

## Sales and Invoicing

The Sales and Invoicing module allows you to process sales transactions and generate invoices.

### Creating a Sale

To create a new sale:

1. Navigate to the Sales page.
2. Click the "Add" button.
3. Fill in the sale details:
   - **Date**: Enter the date of the sale (defaults to today).
   - **Customer**: Select the customer for the sale.
   - **Payment Method**: Select the payment method.
   - **Note**: Enter any notes about the sale (optional).
4. Add products to the sale:
   - Click in the product field and select a product from the dropdown.
   - Enter the quantity.
   - The unit price will be filled automatically based on the product's price or the customer's price list.
   - The total price will be calculated automatically.
   - Repeat for each product in the sale.
5. If needed, apply a discount (see [Applying Discounts](#applying-discounts)).
6. Click the "Save" button.

### Applying Discounts

To apply a discount to a sale:

1. While creating or editing a sale, click on the "Discount %" field.
2. In the discount dialog that appears, enter the discount percentage.
3. Click the "Apply" button.
4. The discount will be applied to the total amount of the sale.

### Processing Payments

To process a payment for a sale:

1. While creating or editing a sale, select the payment method from the dropdown.
2. If the payment method requires additional information (e.g., card number for credit card payments), enter it in the provided fields.
3. Click the "Save" button to complete the sale.

### Generating Invoices

To generate an invoice for a sale:

1. Navigate to the Sales page.
2. Find the sale you want to invoice in the list.
3. Select the sale by clicking on it.
4. Click the "Generate Invoice" button in the toolbar.
5. The system will generate an invoice for the sale and display a success message.

### Printing Receipts

To print a receipt for a sale:

1. Navigate to the Sales page.
2. Find the sale you want to print a receipt for in the list.
3. Select the sale by clicking on it.
4. Click the "Print" button in the toolbar.
5. The system will send the receipt to the default printer.

Alternatively, you can preview the receipt before printing:

1. Navigate to the Sales page.
2. Find the sale you want to preview in the list.
3. Select the sale by clicking on it.
4. Click the "Preview" button in the toolbar.
5. A preview of the receipt will be displayed.
6. Click the "Print" button in the preview to print the receipt.

### Voiding Sales

To void a sale:

1. Navigate to the Sales page.
2. Find the sale you want to void in the list.
3. Select the sale by clicking on it.
4. Click the "Void" button in the toolbar.
5. Enter a reason for voiding the sale in the prompt that appears.
6. Click "OK" to confirm.
7. The sale will be marked as voided and will appear with a "Voided" badge in the list.

### Sales Consultation

To consult sales history:

1. Navigate to the Sales Consultation page.
2. Use the filters to narrow down the sales you want to view:
   - **Date Range**: Select the start and end dates.
   - **Customer**: Select a specific customer (optional).
   - **Payment Method**: Select a specific payment method (optional).
   - **Status**: Select a specific status (e.g., all, voided) (optional).
3. Click the "Search" button.
4. The list will display the sales that match your criteria.
5. Click on a sale to view its details.

## Inventory Management

The Inventory Management module allows you to manage your inventory, including inbound and outbound movements, transfers between warehouses, and kardex.

### Inbound Operations

Inbound operations record products coming into your inventory.

To create a new inbound operation:

1. Navigate to the Inbound page.
2. Click the "Add" button.
3. Fill in the operation details:
   - **Date**: Enter the date of the operation (defaults to today).
   - **Operation**: Select the type of inbound operation (e.g., purchase, return).
   - **Warehouse**: Select the warehouse receiving the products.
   - **Supplier**: Select the supplier providing the products (optional).
   - **Note**: Enter any notes about the operation (optional).
4. Add products to the operation:
   - Click in the product field and select a product from the dropdown.
   - Enter the quantity.
   - Enter the unit price.
   - The total price will be calculated automatically.
   - Repeat for each product in the operation.
5. Click the "Save" button.

### Outbound Operations

Outbound operations record products going out of your inventory.

To create a new outbound operation:

1. Navigate to the Outbound page.
2. Click the "Add" button.
3. Fill in the operation details:
   - **Date**: Enter the date of the operation (defaults to today).
   - **Operation**: Select the type of outbound operation (e.g., loss, damage).
   - **Warehouse**: Select the warehouse the products are leaving from.
   - **Note**: Enter any notes about the operation (optional).
4. Add products to the operation:
   - Click in the product field and select a product from the dropdown.
   - Enter the quantity.
   - Repeat for each product in the operation.
5. Click the "Save" button.

### Warehouse Transfers

Warehouse transfers record products moving from one warehouse to another.

To create a new warehouse transfer:

1. Navigate to the Transfers page.
2. Click the "Add" button.
3. Fill in the transfer details:
   - **Date**: Enter the date of the transfer (defaults to today).
   - **Source Warehouse**: Select the warehouse the products are leaving from.
   - **Destination Warehouse**: Select the warehouse the products are going to.
   - **Note**: Enter any notes about the transfer (optional).
4. Click the "Save" button.

### Kardex

The Kardex provides a detailed record of all inventory movements for a specific product.

To view the kardex for a product:

1. Navigate to the Kardex page.
2. Select the product you want to view the kardex for.
3. Select the warehouse (optional).
4. Select the date range (optional).
5. Click the "Search" button.
6. The kardex will display all movements for the selected product, including:
   - Date
   - Operation type
   - Quantity in
   - Quantity out
   - Balance
   - Unit price
   - Total price

## Cash Management

The Cash Management module allows you to manage your cash registers, cash shifts, and cash movements.

### Cash Registers

To manage cash registers:

1. Navigate to the Cash Registers page.
2. To add a new cash register, click the "Add" button and fill in the cash register details.
3. To edit a cash register, find it in the list, click the "Edit" button, modify the details, and click "Save".
4. To delete a cash register, find it in the list, click the "Delete" button, and confirm the deletion.

### Opening Cash Shifts

To open a cash shift:

1. Navigate to the Cash Register Dashboard.
2. Find the cash register you want to open a shift for.
3. Click the "Open Shift" button.
4. Enter the initial cash amount.
5. Click the "Open" button.
6. The cash shift will be opened and you'll be redirected to the Cash Movements page.

### Cash Movements

To record cash movements:

1. Navigate to the Cash Movements page for the open cash shift.
2. To add a cash in movement, click the "Cash In" button, fill in the details, and click "Save".
3. To add a cash out movement, click the "Cash Out" button, fill in the details, and click "Save".
4. The list will display all cash movements for the current shift.

### Closing Cash Shifts

To close a cash shift:

1. Navigate to the Cash Register Dashboard.
2. Find the cash register with the open shift you want to close.
3. Click the "Close Shift" button.
4. Enter the final cash count.
5. The system will calculate the difference between the expected amount and the actual count.
6. Enter any notes about the discrepancy (if any).
7. Click the "Close" button.
8. The cash shift will be closed and you'll be redirected to the Cash Register Dashboard.

### Cash Consultation

To consult cash history:

1. Navigate to the Cash Consultation page.
2. Use the filters to narrow down the cash shifts you want to view:
   - **Date Range**: Select the start and end dates.
   - **Cash Register**: Select a specific cash register (optional).
   - **User**: Select a specific user (optional).
   - **Status**: Select a specific status (e.g., open, closed) (optional).
3. Click the "Search" button.
4. The list will display the cash shifts that match your criteria.
5. Click on a cash shift to view its details and movements.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### The system is slow

- Check your internet connection.
- Close unnecessary browser tabs and applications.
- Clear your browser cache and cookies.
- Try using a different browser.

#### I can't log in

- Make sure you're entering the correct username and password.
- Check if Caps Lock is on.
- If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link to reset it.
- Contact your system administrator if you continue to have issues.

#### I can't find a feature

- Check if you have the necessary permissions to access the feature.
- Look for the feature in the side navigation menu.
- Use the search function if available.
- Consult this user manual for information on where to find specific features.

### Error Messages

#### "Insufficient stock"

This error occurs when you try to sell or transfer more of a product than is available in the selected warehouse. Check the current stock level and adjust the quantity accordingly.

#### "Invalid customer information"

This error occurs when the customer information is incomplete or invalid. Make sure all required fields are filled in correctly.

#### "Printer not found"

This error occurs when the system can't find the selected printer. Make sure the printer is connected and properly configured.

## Glossary

- **Branch**: A physical location of your business.
- **IGV**: Impuesto General a las Ventas (General Sales Tax) in Peru.
- **Kardex**: A detailed record of inventory movements for a specific product.
- **POS**: Point of Sale, the location where a retail transaction is completed.
- **RUC**: Registro Único de Contribuyentes (Unique Taxpayer Registry) in Peru.
- **Warehouse**: A physical location where inventory is stored.
